Prepare an Election

Manage Eligibility

Add participant wallet addresses during the Commit phase. Only addresses on the eligibility list can vote, and only once per election.

Optional Exclusion Controls

Excluding a participant blocks them from voting. Reinstating them removes the exclusion but does not add them back to the eligibility list.

Election Lifecycle

End voting before participants reveal. Finalisation publishes the vote counts, the audit list and a result hash on-chain. Participants who do not reveal are recorded as not revealed and are not counted. After finalising, you can start a new election directly or reset to Setup first. Past results are always kept.

Published Results and Audit

Leave the election ID empty to load the current election's results. Enter a previous election ID to view its saved results and audit. Results are available only after finalisation.
